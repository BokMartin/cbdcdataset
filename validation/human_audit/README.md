# Sampled human validation v10.2e

This directory preserves the frozen double-blind validation boundary and the completed independent pre-adjudication audit used by the paper.

- Population: 6,949 deduplicated, source-verified candidates.
- Common candidate sample: 365 claims (5.2526%), independently reviewed by Martin and Dominik.
- Separate dual-empty audit: 36 of 359 units reviewed by both authors.
- Design: deterministic SHA-256 selection without replacement, stratified by language and provider-origin type.
- Precision target: two-sided 95% interval with a worst-case finite-population-corrected half-width of 4.993 percentage points.

The two original reviewer workbooks contain the same cases in the same blind order:

- `VALIDATION_SAMPLE_v10_2e_MARTIN.xlsx`
- `VALIDATION_SAMPLE_v10_2e_DOMINIK.xlsx`

`SAMPLED_VALIDATION_FREEZE_MANIFEST.json` records the sampling design and frozen hashes. `SAMPLED_VALIDATION_WORKBOOK_VERIFICATION.json` records workbook structure and pre-review hashes; the reviewer manifests record the externally distributed blind packages. The historical workbook-generation toolchain is intentionally omitted because it is not required to reproduce the released paper results.

Both authors completed the review independently under the blind protocol and confirmed the blind status on 27 August 2026. The released analysis uses 364 analyzable candidates after excluding `SV-0250`, which had no usable translation. All confidence values are treated as `high`, and exclusion reasons are not analyzed.

Release artifacts:

- `reviewed_candidates.csv`: paired human decisions and coding fields for the common sample.
- `adjudication_queue.csv`: 319 cases with at least one unresolved substantive disagreement.
- `dual_empty_joint_audit.csv`: joint audit of the 36 sampled both-empty units; five untranslated units are excluded from analysis.
- `dual_empty_supplements.csv`: two recovered claims from `DE-0009`.
- `VALIDATION_v10_2e_HUMAN_AUDIT_PREADJUDICATION.xlsx`: review-friendly combined workbook with `confidence = high` throughout.
- `../../results/human_validation/human_validation_results.json`: deterministic estimates generated by `../../scripts/analyze_human_validation_v10_2e.py`.

The independent phase is complete, but consensus adjudication remains work in progress. Reviewer-specific design-weighted validity is 78.6% for Martin and 76.1% for Dominik; their 77.3% midpoint is descriptive rather than a final consensus estimate. Raw inclusion agreement is 62.6% (228/364), with Gwet's AC1 of 0.426 and Krippendorff's nominal alpha of -0.069.
