# v10.2e exploratory assisted production amendment

Date frozen: 2026-08-25

The protocol-exact v10.2 development calibration is preserved unchanged. The source-verified union achieved probability precision 0.980, recall 0.766, and span fidelity 1.000, but failed the predeclared E1 and E2 retrieval gates and C1 exact-code gate. This amendment does not reinterpret those failures as passes and does not authorize an automated extraction or classification claim.

The full-corpus run is an exploratory LLM-assisted candidate-generation workflow. OpenAI and Claude receive the same source units, prompt, codebook, authority metadata, schema, renders, and substantive settings. Raw provider outputs are sealed before cross-model comparison. Unsupported quotations are rejected after one source-only retry. The sealed 40-page reserve is not included, opened, or used.

All 113 included documents and all 3,963 pages enter preparation without semantic prefiltering. Pages are processed in document and page order. Long page text may be split mechanically with the frozen language-sensitive token budget and overlap. Pages marked `ocr_needed=True` in the existing page manifest receive a frozen grayscale render in addition to any extracted text.

The source-verified provider outputs are combined by deterministic one-to-one span matching at the unchanged 0.80 overlap threshold. Every deduplicated candidate receives a human inclusion decision and final code. Candidates are allocated between Martin and Dominik so that every candidate is reviewed by at least one author. A deterministic stratified 20% overlap is independently coded by both authors before consensus. A deterministic stratified 10% sample of units where both models return no candidate is also independently reviewed by both authors. Human-supplemented claims are retained with `origin=human_supplement`.

The paper will report the frozen calibration metrics, human acceptance/rejection of LLM candidates, exact LLM-to-final-code agreement, pre-consensus Martin-Dominik agreement, and the yield of the dual-empty-unit audit. Substantive results use the human-adjudicated dataset. The paper will call the process exploratory and LLM-assisted, not validated automated extraction or automated classification.
