# AI master seal

The full and reviewer-specific AI candidate masters were generated before human coding and stored outside Git. Only their SHA-256 seal is tracked here, so the blind review files remain free of provider labels and AI decisions.

Do not disclose the masters, mapping, previews, or archive until both independent human-review workbooks are returned and hash-locked. After that lock, the sample masters can be joined by `sample_case_id` for pre-consensus agreement and provider-validity analysis.

The full master contains the entire deduplicated provider union. The reviewer-specific masters contain the frozen probability sample and are identical except for the reviewer label. These files support candidate-validity and coding-agreement estimates; production recall remains a separate calibration estimand.
