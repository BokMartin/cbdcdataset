How it is measured

Exact evidence first. Every accepted statement has a source-verifiable span. Unsupported spans are rejected; source-only retry is allowed once.

One candidate, one vote. Each deduplicated candidate contributes total mass 1. Shared candidates split that mass across providers and distinct codes, so duplicate model output cannot increase weight.

Signed posture. Privacy direction is combined with whether the design affects visibility from the state or from intermediaries, its observation/decision/recommendation status and evidence strength. Decisions receive full ODR weight and proposals half weight.

Important limit. Calibration precision was 0.980 and span fidelity 1.000, but union recall was 0.766 and failed the frozen recall gate. The blind audit produced reviewer-specific validity estimates of 78.6% and 76.1%, yet inclusion agreement was 62.6%. These production values are therefore exploratory machine-assisted measures, not automated human gold.
